# Elecrow Due Diligence for Glimlings

## The Verdict

**Elecrow is worth pursuing. The services exist. The complete workflow is not yet proven.**

---

## 1. Lead Times: The Biggest Christmas Risk

| Stage | Published | What It Means |
|-------|-----------|---------------|
| Initial quotation | 24 hours for PCBA RFQs | Response target, not completion promise |
| Individual 3D print | 1-3 days | Relevant to approved shell |
| PCB assembly | 3 weeks general | Avoid new PCB for Christmas |
| Dispatch stocked products | 1-3 business days | Not for custom bundles |
| International post | 7-25 days | Unsuitable for last-minute gifts |
| DHL | 3-5 business days transit | Manufacturing + customs additional |

**No published end-to-end lead time for personalised, assembled Glimling.**

**Solution:** Have Elecrow stock M5Stack modules and sensors. Each order only needs printing, assembly, testing and packing.

---

## 2. Customer Reports

**Trustpilot: 1.9/5 across 21 reviews (mixed)**

- April 2026: In-stock order delayed 1+ month
- June 2026: Custom order not shipped after 3 months
- September 2026: Replacement for faulty device received

**Reddit (r/PrintedCircuitBoard): Mixed**
- Some: Excellent sourcing, responsive account managers
- Others: Poor small-prototype experiences, inconsistent instructions

**Lesson:** Named project manager + written test procedure = better results.

---

## 3. Real Customer Project: PocketPD

**February 2026:** Elecrow completed hardware manufacturing ahead of deadline.

**April 2026:** Products reached Mouser for fulfilment.

**Problem:** Unclear specs led to 600+ units needing rework (same enclosure for two variants).

**Lesson:** Give each custom shell an immutable design ID. Worker scans shell + electronics before packaging.

---

## 4. Costs and Minimum-Order Traps

| Item | Published | Problem |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Minimum quantity | 1 piece PCBA | Doesn't guarantee profitable one-off kit |
| Promotional printing | $1 first project/day | Not recurring production cost |
| Regular printing | Quoted by weight | Each character may differ |
| Dropshipping | $3 base | Not total fulfilment charge |
| Order submission | Manual or website | No verified Shopify API |

**Dropshipping formula:** Shipping × 1.1 + $1.50 + packaging + labour

**$3 advertised ≠ $3 all-inclusive.**

---

## 5. What Could Derail Christmas

### Legal Responsibility

- Selling under own brand = manufacturer under UK radio rules
- Compliant M5Stack ≠ compliant finished product
- Connected-device security regime applies
- Outsaking assembly ≠ transferring obligations

### Import Taxes

- UK: VAT at point of sale for goods ≤£135
- Shopify doesn't automatically handle VAT
- Get delivered-duty-paid shipping options

### Product Functionality

- Elecrow assembles without knowing if firmware works
- Give simple pass/fail test procedure
- Verify: hardware revision, mic, speaker, lighting, firmware, onboarding

## 7. The Decision

**Pursue Elecrow. Don't make it single point of failure.**

### Initial Test

Two different personalised shells, same electronics, same sensor:
- One to UK address
- One to different country
- Tests: unique designs, inventory coordination, firmware verification, packing, international delivery

### Parallel Fallback

- Shell-only Shopify product
- Printed and fulfilled independently
- Commercially useful if complete-kit too expensive

### The Positive Finding

Elecrow has real experience producing and packaging complete third-party electronics.

### The Unresolved Question

Can it deliver one different, beautifully finished, functional Glimling per order at the cost and reliability your gift business requires?

**One paid end-to-end sample and a written operational quotation will settle this.**
