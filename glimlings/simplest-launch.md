## The Simplest Launch

1. **Sell:** Personalised shell through Studio + Etsy
2. **Recommend:** One voice module + one sensor from regional retailer
3. **Provide:** Free firmware, garden onboarding, plant MCP service
4. **Add later:** One-box ready-to-use gift

---

## Why NOT Bundle Immediately

- Someone who already owns hardware only needs the shell
- Someone who wants voice but no plants only needs the voice module
- Studio assembles the appropriate purchasing guide for each customer
- Don't charge for unnecessary parts

---

## The £1 Generic Probe Problem

**Keep it out of initial consumer offering.** It's an analogue component requiring a compatible controller and wiring—not a standalone wireless sensor.
