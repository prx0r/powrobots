```
Subject: Glimlings Kit — Complete Sensor & Hardware Enquiry

Hi Elecrow,

We're developing Glimlings, personalised garden creatures.

For each product, we need:
1. Can you source these exact SKUs?
2. What's the per-unit cost at 1, 10, 50 orders?
3. Can you bundle them with our custom 3D-printed shells?

HARDWARE:
- M5Stack Atom Voice C008-C (voice module)

PLANT SENSORS:
- HHCCJCY01 or HHCCJCY10 (Flower Care / MiFlora, Bluetooth)
- Seeed XIAO wireless soil sensor

ENVIRONMENT SENSORS:
- DHT22 / AM2302 (temperature + humidity)
- BME280 (temperature + humidity + pressure)
- BH1750 (light level)

WATER SENSORS:
- Water leak sensor module
- Water level sensor

MOTION SENSORS:
- PIR motion sensor
- Reed switch (door/window)

Please quote for each sensor at 1, 10, and 50 units.
Include: component cost, sourcing fee, shipping to your warehouse.

Thanks,
POW
```
