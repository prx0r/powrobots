# The Fulfilment Solution: Sell Packs, Not Individual Sensors

## The Core Insight

**Bundle cheap sensors into a kit. Let a fulfilment partner hold inventory. Don't store, assemble or ship anything yourself.**

A £1 capacitive moisture probe has no wireless connection, battery or waterproof enclosure. It needs to connect to something. Selling it individually makes little sense.

## Three Fulfilment Arrangements

### 1. Made to Order

- Elecrow sources hardware + prints shell after each order
- Minimal upfront commitment
- Higher cost, longer lead time

### 2. Small Stock (Recommended)

- You purchase modest number of voice modules + sensors
- Elecrow stores them
- Produces custom shells to order

### 3. Complete Kits

- Stock pretested sensor-and-electronics packs
- Personalised shell printed per customer
- After demand is established
