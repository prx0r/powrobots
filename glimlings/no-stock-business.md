## The No-Stock Model

### What We Hold

- Shells (from printing partner, print on demand)
- Bundles (shell + sensor, small inventory)
- Accessories (seasonal caps, etc.)

### What We Don't Hold

- M5Stack electronics
- Individual sensors
- Bulk inventory

### Why This Works

1. **No electronics inventory risk** — Customer buys locally
2. **No sensor shipping** — Customer buys from Amazon/AliExpress
3. **Print on demand** — Shells made per order
4. **Small bundle inventory** — Only hold what we ship
5. **Low capital** — No expensive electronics stock
