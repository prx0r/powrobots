## Recommendation

**Offer BOTH options:**

| Option | Price | Target Customer |
|--------|-------|-----------------|
| **Snap-In Kit** | £39.99-79.99 | Hobbyists, gifts, education |
| **Factory Assembled** | £69.99-149.99 | Convenience, premium |

**Start with snap-in kits:**
1. Cheaper to produce
2. Faster to ship (5-9 days vs 15-36 days)
3. Higher margins
4. Test demand before investing in factory assembly

**Then add factory assembled:**
1. Once you have 50+ orders
2. Contact Makerfabs for bulk assembly
3. Offer as premium option

---

## The Key Insight

**Snap-in kits are cheaper, faster, and higher margin than factory assembly.**

The customer does 1-5 minutes of simple snapping (no soldering). You save 50-60% on manufacturing costs.

**Start with snap-in. Add factory later.**
