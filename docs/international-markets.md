# International Markets: US, EU, and Beyond

## Can We Launch in US and Other Markets?

**YES — but with different radio frequencies, plug types, and compliance.**

---

## Radio Frequencies by Market

| Market | Radio | Plug | Compliance |
|--------|-------|------|------------|
| UK | 868MHz | Type G | UK PSTI, UKCA |
| US | 915MHz | Type A/B | FCC, UL |
| EU | 868MHz | Type C/F | CE, RoHS |
| Australia | 433MHz | Type I | RCM |
| Canada | 915MHz | Type A/B | IC, CSA |

---

## The US Market Opportunity

### Why US

- 67% of US households have plants (same as UK)
- Larger population (67M plant owners)
- Higher disposable income
- Strong plant parent culture
- Etsy has huge US customer base

### Challenges

- Different radio frequency (915MHz vs 868MHz)
- Different plug type (Type A/B vs Type G)
- FCC compliance required
- Shipping from UK is expensive
- Need US-based fulfilment

### Solution

- Use same electronics (just different radio module)
- Use same enclosure (just different plug adapter)
- Partner with US fulfilment centre
- Or: Sell digital products only (app, subscriptions)

---

## The EU Market Opportunity

### Why EU

- 868MHz same as UK (no radio change needed)
- Large plant parent market
- Strong gift culture
- EU compliance (CE) is simpler than UK (UKCA)

### Challenges

- Different plug type (Type C/F vs Type G)
- EU compliance (CE marking)
- VAT registration in each country
- Shipping from UK may face customs

### Solution

- Use same electronics (868MHz works in EU)
- Add EU plug adapter
- CE marking instead of UKCA
- Ship from EU fulfilment centre

---

## The Australia Market Opportunity

### Why Australia

- Strong plant parent culture
- High disposable income
- English-speaking (same marketing)
- Large indoor plant market

### Challenges

- Different radio frequency (433MHz)
- Different plug type (Type I)
- RCM compliance required
- Shipping from UK is expensive

### Solution

- Use same electronics (just different radio module)
- Use same enclosure (just different plug adapter)
- RCM compliance
- Partner with AU fulfilment centre

## Key Insight

**Start with UK. Expand to US and EU.**

- UK: Test concept, prove demand
- US: Larger market, higher prices
- EU: Same radio as UK, simpler expansion
- Australia: English-speaking, but different radio

**The product is the same. Only radio, plug, and compliance change.**
