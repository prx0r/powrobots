# Christmas 2026 Product Strategy

## The Opportunity

Garden Glimlings is one of POW's strongest Christmas product opportunities. The opportunity isn't simply selling plant monitors. It's selling personalised, collectible little creatures that people can assemble, give names to and watch react to their plants.

---

## The Cost Problem

Your own engine supplier matrix estimates £49.10 delivered cost for a SENSE product, while Sporebert's proposed retail price is £44.99. Those estimates are unverified, but they contradict the earlier 28% profit projection.

Etsy's UK fees include 6.5% transaction fees and 4% plus £0.20 payment processing.

**The immediate commercial task is not designing eight different electronics assemblies. It's defining one giftable design with an achievable manufacturing-cost ceiling.**

## The Christmas Gift: Adopt a Glimling

The recipient opens a little illustrated box containing:
- Preassembled electronic heart
- Character body
- Personalised head
- Character card

They snap it together, plug it in and watch it come alive.

They can change its personality, connect it to their AI assistant or simply enjoy its physical reactions. Later, they can collect alternative heads and accessories.

**The packaging and unboxing experience could matter as much as the plant-monitoring feature.**

---

## The Christmas-Specific Opportunity

### Seasonal Accessories, Not Seasonal Electronics

A Christmas Sporebert could include:
- Snow-covered mushroom cap
- Tiny scarf
- Personalised gift message

In January, the recipient swaps back to a normal cap. **You haven't created an electronic product that becomes irrelevant after Christmas.**

### Seasonal Calendar

| Occasion | Product | Strategy |
|----------|---------|----------|
| Christmas | Sporebert + seasonal caps | Gift boxes |
| Valentine's Day | Friendship Spirits | Matching pair |
| Mother's Day | Petals | Flower theme |
| Birthdays | Character-specific boxes | Year-round |

### Low-Cost Entry Product

Non-electronic personalised mushroom pot marker or decorative character kit. Not as differentiated, but provides alternative if electronics can't be manufactured economically this year.

---

## The Decision for Christmas 2026

### Concentrate On

1. **One standard electronic core** — Sporebert hardware
2. **Small interchangeable-head collection** — 3-4 heads
3. **POW Studio configuration** — Personalise the gift

### Develop in Parallel

1. **Fern** — Demonstrate in videos, develop mechanically
2. **Desk Goblin** — Test demand outside plant audience
3. **Seasonal accessories** — Low-cost add-ons

### Don't Depend On

1. **Fern for initial Christmas fulfillment** — Mechanical complexity
2. **Eight different electronics assemblies** — Too ambitious
3. **Connected features** — Compliance requirements

---

## The Commercial Checkpoints

For Q4 starting October 1:

1. **Appealing demonstration** — Video of Sporebert glowing
2. **Verified manufacturing route** — JLCPCB + JLC3DP confirmed
3. **Honest delivery dates** — Realistic lead times
4. **Viable contribution margin** — Positive after fees

### If Compliance or Production Can't Be Completed

Keep the commercial offer to:
- Ready-to-fulfil non-electronic designs
- Seasonal accessories
- Don't promise hardware that hasn't been validated

**UK radio products have applicable conformity requirements.**
**Etsy requires disclosure of third-party production partners.**

---

## The Bigger Picture

Garden Glimlings is a promising consumer acquisition wedge, but not necessarily POW's most valuable long-term business. Robot repair and procurement still have stronger connections to POW's specialised parts and outcome data.

**Glimlings gives you a way to develop that manufacturing intelligence using products you control.**

---

## The Play

```
1. Define one giftable design (Sporebert)
2. Verify manufacturing cost ceiling
3. Build 10 prototypes
4. Test on Etsy
5. Collect feedback
6. Iterate for Christmas

Fern is the demo product.
Sporebert is the Christmas product.
The platform is the long-term product.
```
