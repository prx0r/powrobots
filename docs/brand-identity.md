# Brand Identity — Glimlings

Little spirits for the things you love.

---

## The brand

**Glimlings** — tiny magical creatures that inhabit everyday objects and give them a presence in your digital world.

**A Glimling** — one individual creature.

**Glimlings** — the collectible family.

The Shakespearean inspiration: Titania's attendants Peaseblossom, Cobweb, Moth and Mustardseed. Almost ready-made names for garden creatures.

## The aesthetic

Tiny enchanted woodland. Not plastic robot toys.

- Acorn hats
- Oversized ears
- Crooked smiles
- Translucent wings
- Glowing bellies
- Little boots
- Slightly mischievous expressions

**The technology should feel like the magic hidden inside the creature.**

---

## Naming rules

- Brand: **Glimlings** (plural — collectible family)
- Individual: **A Glimling** (singular — each has identity)
- Product: **Garden Glimlings**, **Sleep Glimlings**, etc.
- Voice: "Check on my Glimlings" / "How's my Glimling doing?"
- Expansion: Naturally accommodates multiple devices

### Avoid

- **Whimlings** — already used by Whimside game and handmade creature collection
- **Piskies** — Cornish folklore term, used commercially for figurines
- **Pisky** — established Cornish term, trademark risk

---

## The world

A Glimling is a tiny magical creature that inhabits an everyday object and gives it a presence in your digital world.

A customer's Glimlings could include:
- **Mosswick** — lives in the garden, watches the tomatoes
- **Mab** — lives on the bedside table, manages alarms
- **Puck** — lives on the desk, carries out AI workflows
- **Boomoo** — lives in the flowerpot, particularly silly

Their assistant can ask about any Glimling by name. Each has its own memory, personality and relationship with its owner.
