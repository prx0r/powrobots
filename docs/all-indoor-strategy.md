# All-Indoor Strategy: Simpler, Cheaper, Faster

## Why Indoor First

- No waterproofing needed (saves £5-10 per unit)
- No gateway needed (Bluetooth connects direct)
- Simpler assembly
- Faster to market
- Lower risk
- Bigger market (67% of UK households have indoor plants)

**The play:** One Glimling manages ALL your indoor plants. Existing Bluetooth sensors connect directly. No custom hardware needed for sensors. Our moat is the SOFTWARE and EXPERIENCE.
