### Setup Guide

```
1. Buy M5Stack Atom Voice from local retailer
2. Download Glimlings firmware
3. Flash firmware to device
4. Connect to WiFi
5. Pair with your AI agent
6. Clip into your Glimling shell
7. Done!
```

## About Page

### What Are Glimlings?

"Glimlings are small, personalised creatures that bring your AI agent into the physical world. They glow, speak, and react — making your digital assistant feel present in your room."

### How They Work

1. Choose a character (mushroom, frog, ghost, cloud)
2. Personalise it (colour, name, expression)
3. Connect to your AI agent (Muse, ChatGPT, Home Assistant)
4. Your Glimling glows and speaks when your agent has something to say

### The Garden Theme

"Our characters are inspired by the magical creatures that inhabit gardens and forests. Each Glimling is a tiny spirit that looks after your space — whether that's monitoring plants, greeting you at the door, or simply glowing quietly on your desk."

### Privacy & Security

- Local processing (no cloud required for basic functions)
- Microphone-off switch with visible indicator
- No always-listening without explicit opt-in
- UK PSTI compliance
- Open-source firmware option
