## Recommendation

### For prototyping (1 unit):
1. **Use Seeed Fusion** - £189-299 per unit
2. **Or contact Prism/M-TEK** - might be cheaper for UK
3. **Accept the loss** - it's R&D, not production

### For small batch (10 units):
1. **Use UK Electronics** - MOQ 10, better per-unit cost
2. **Or Seeed Fusion** - still viable at 10 units

### For production (50+ units):
1. **Use Seeed Fusion ODM** - £15-20 per unit
2. **Or JLCPCB + UK Electronics** - split the work
