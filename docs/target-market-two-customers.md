# Target Market: Two Customers, One Brand

## The Two Customers

| Customer | What They Want | What They Buy |
|----------|----------------|---------------|
| **Plant lovers & gift buyers** | Adorable, personalised garden sprite that lights up, talks, looks after plants | One finished, ready-to-use Glimling |
| **Makers & AI enthusiasts** | Beautiful enclosure and easy software for hardware they already own | Custom shell, firmware, compatibility guides |

**First = consumer brand. Second = how you start selling with minimal capital.**

---

## The Garden as Brand, Not Technical Limitation

Glimlings = mushrooms, fairies, woodland spirits, frogs, garden guardians.

- Live beside plants, on desk, next to bed
- Coherent visual identity
- Doesn't restrict what hardware can do
- Much clearer audience than "MCP compatibility platform"

## The Design Moat

AI-generated 3D models are easily replicated. Stronger asset:

- Library of distinctive, manufacturable characters
- Each validated against specific electronic modules
- Successful prints, material tolerances, production prices
- Customer preferences and compatibility records
- Connects to wider POW procurement graph

---

## The First Product

> "Design your own little woodland spirit. Give it a name, a personality and a home beside your plants."

- Choose ready-to-use gift OR shell for M5Stack you already own
- Creature can glow and speak through supported software
- Optional garden abilities with compatible sensors

**Strongest demonstration:** Tiny mushroom that glows when you approach and quietly says something useful about your plants. Person watching shouldn't need to know ESP32, MCP gateway or moisture sensor exists.

**That is the consumer experience worth validating. The compatibility platform makes it affordable and expandable behind the scenes.**
