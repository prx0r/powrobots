# What Should We Offer? — Canonical Review

## Can We Slap a Logo and Sell as Our Own?

**Technically YES, but it's risky and potentially illegal.**

### The Risks

1. **Etsy rules:** Reselling commercially available products is prohibited
2. **UK law:** Selling radio equipment under your trademark = you're the manufacturer
3. **Compliance:** You're responsible for FCC/CE/UKCA compliance
4. **WEEE:** You're responsible for waste electrical equipment
5. **Product liability:** If it breaks, you're liable

### The Better Play

**Don't resell. VALIDATE and LINK.**

- Test sensors work with Glimlings
- Add "Glimling Compatible" badge
- Link to where to buy them
- Earn referral fees
- No compliance burden
- No inventory risk

## The Recommendation

### Sell on Etsy

- Sporebert hub (original design)
- Interchangeable heads (original design)
- Seasonal accessories (original design)
- Starter kits (if original design dominant)

### Sell on glimlings.app

- Glimling Compatible sensor links
- Third-party sensor referrals
- App subscriptions
- Accessory marketplace

### Don't Do This

- ❌ Don't resell HHCC sensors with a logo
- ❌ Don't resell Ecowitt sensors with a logo
- ❌ Don't claim compliance we haven't achieved
- ❌ Don't violate Etsy's reselling rules

## The Play

```
Etsy: Sell original POW products
Website: Sell the ecosystem (sensors, accessories, subscriptions)
Both: Earn referral fees from sensor sales
```

**The moat is the PLATFORM, not the hardware.**
