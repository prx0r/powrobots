## Key Insights

### 1. Indoor Starter is the Best Product

- 41% margin
- Simple assembly (Bluetooth sensor, no gateway)
- Works out of the box
- Natural upgrade path

### 2. Outdoor Starter is Challenging

- 24% margin (thin)
- Needs gateway (£40 cost)
- Ecowitt pricing limits margin
- Waterproofing adds complexity

### 3. Extra Sensors Have Mixed Economics

- Indoor sensor: 22% margin (viable)
- Outdoor sensor: -1% margin (unviable at current pricing)

**Solution:** Develop custom sensors or negotiate wholesale discounts.

### 4. Interchangeable Heads are High-Margin

- 6-30% margin
- Low cost to produce
- High perceived value
- Collectibility driver

### 5. The Real Revenue is in Software

| Revenue Stream | Source |
|----------------|--------|
| Starter kits | One-time hardware sale |
| Expansion sensors | Accessory sales |
| Interchangeable heads | Collectible accessories |
| **Glimlings app** | **Subscription potential** |
| **MCP integration** | **AI agent value** |
| **Plant care data** | **Long-term intelligence** |
