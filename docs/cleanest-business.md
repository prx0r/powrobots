# The Cleanest Business: Sell One Glimling, Build a Plant Homelab

## The Play

1. **Hero product:** Sporebert Indoor Starter.
2. **Add-on economy:** heads, caps, stands, aesthetic packs, labels, seasonal bundles.
3. **Open compatible network:** "works with" third-party sensors.
4. **Later:** your own cheaper indoor Plantling node once you've validated demand and know which measurements people actually care about.

That gives you a very strong wedge: the customer buys a cute gift once, then "extends the family" over time instead of being forced into buying another whole Glimling each time.

---

## Channel Strategy

### Etsy

Use Etsy for:

- Sporebert Starter
- Sporebert standalone
- Custom caps / heads / aesthetic packs
- Gift bundles
- Personalised packaging

**Do not use Etsy as a storefront for random Ecowitt / HHCC / AliExpress sensors by themselves.** That is the exact area where Etsy policy becomes dangerous.

### Your Own Site

Use your own site for:

- "Glimling Compatible" directory
- Cheapest supplier by region
- Amazon / manufacturer / AliExpress / specialist retailer links
- Affiliate / referral revenue
- "Bring your own sensor" setup guides
- Advanced compatibility tables

That's where the real POW angle comes in: not just selling hardware, but being **the canonical compatibility layer** for plant gear.

## Sensor Network: Three Tiers

### Tier 1: Must-Have Network Sensors

| Sensor | What It Does | Benchmark |
|--------|--------------|-----------|
| Soil moisture | Core per-plant measurement | Ecowitt WH51 ($17.99) |
| Advanced soil probe | Moisture + temp + EC | Ecowitt WH52 ($27.99) |
| Leak detection | Water leak alarm | Ecowitt WH55 ($15.99) |
| Leaf wetness | Greenhouse / disease risk | Ecowitt WN35 ($54.99) |

### Tier 2: Environment / Ambience Sensors

| Sensor | What It Does | Benchmark |
|--------|--------------|-----------|
| 5-in-1 environment | Temp, humidity, pressure, light, UV | DFRobot SEN0501 ($29.90) |
| Rainfall | Garden rainfall measurement | DFRobot SEN0575 |
| Water level | Reservoir monitoring | Seeed Grove Water Level |
| CO2 / temp / humidity | Greenhouse monitoring | Seeed Grove SCD41 ($52.90) |

### Tier 3: Interaction / Personality Sensors

| Sensor | What It Does | Benchmark |
|--------|--------------|-----------|
| Motion detection | "Glimling notices you" | Seeed Grove PIR ($8.70) |
| Ambient sound | Room quiet/noisy detection | DFRobot Analog Sound Sensor |
| Light / day-night | Day vs night behaviour | SEN0501 (included) |
| Vision AI / camera | Time-lapses, garden watching | Seeed Grove Vision AI V2 |

## The "Whispering" Behaviour

```
PIR sees motion
    +
Ambient light says it's evening
    +
Sound sensor says room is quiet
    ↓
Glimling says: "hey... basil's thirsty"
    or
Glimling says: "your monstera is happy today"
```

That feels magical. It turns the Glimling from "dashboard" into **presence-aware companion.**

**Requires:** Speaker-capable hub variant (TALK-enabled Sporebert)

## Commercial Model

### Phase 1: Original Glimling + Curated Compatibility

- Sell Sporebert
- Sell heads / caps / design packs
- Support selected third-party sensors
- List "best buy" options by region
- Earn affiliate / referral income
- Build the compatibility graph

### Phase 2: Bundle Selected Third-Party Sensors

- Only after reliable supply path
- Only in branded starter kits
- Not as arbitrary standalone dropship
- Ideally co-packed in one giftable box

### Phase 3: Your Own Sensor Line

- Build indoor Plantling node first
- Do outdoor later (waterproofing is harder)
- Know which measurements matter most
